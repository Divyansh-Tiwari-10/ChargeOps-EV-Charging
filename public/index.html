CHARGEOPS GUIDE

How to use ChargeOps.

Follow these steps to find a public charger or try the sample booking workflow.

  1. 01

    Find nearby chargers

    In Explore, search for a city or area across India. Or choose Use my location and allow location access in your browser to search around you.

  2. 02

    Check the public listing

    Listings combine OpenStreetMap records and a BEE dataset snapshot. Check directions, then confirm the station is open, working, compatible, and current with its operator. Live availability and current tariffs aren’t provided here.

  3. 03

    Add your EV and try a booking

    Use Your EVs to add a vehicle. Public stations aren’t connected to operator booking systems; to try the flow, open See India sample hubs, choose a sample station, select a compatible port and time, then confirm the simulated booking.

  4. 04

    Review your charging history

    Choose Charging history to view bookings, sessions, and invoices. Sample locations, rates, reservations, and payments are simulated and don’t reserve or charge a real station.
